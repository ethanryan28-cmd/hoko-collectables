**Publishers:**
- YouTube: resumable upload with `status.publishAt` (private until then), all items within 14 days at once, OAuth refresh token.
- Facebook: `/videos` with `published=false` + `scheduled_publish_time` (15 min–14 days ahead).

- Discord: webhook embed; TikTok pack = header + one message per clip with the mp4 attached and its caption.
- TikTok: Content Posting API (FILE_UPLOAD) behind `TIKTOK_ENABLED=false`, privacy `SELF_ONLY` until the app is audited.

**Runner:**
- `data/social-post-log.csv` is the idempotency key.
- A failure is logged RETRY, then RETRY again, then FAIL (2 retries then FAIL).
- More than 6 h past its slot → SKIP (never posts late).
- Missing keys → platform reported NOT CONFIGURED and items stay pending.
- Dry-run builds and validates every payload with no network → `data/social-dryrun.json`.

**Queue rules:**
- Sources: product reels, tips, roundups, BF/Christmas beats (pinned to their date). It also picks up Shorts cuts / CardDrop / social-bank when present.
- Promotes only ACTIVE, non-OOS-SUPPLIER products with inventory > 0. This tightened today: all live variants are DENY, so 0 stock is unbuyable (see 2i).
- Japanese sealed ≈70% through November, 50% in December.
- No clip/pin repeats on the same platform within 7 days, and each product appears at most once per platform per day.
- Topup keeps already-scheduled items untouched.

**Captions:** written in-session by templates with no API calls. Each ends "Collector first. Seller second.", uses "Victoria, Australia", and buy posts carry the six-game line. No prices; no Whatnot / mystery pack / official / Top Rated; no handle changes (`check` enforces all of this).
