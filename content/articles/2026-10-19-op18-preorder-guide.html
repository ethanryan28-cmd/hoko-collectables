What OP-18 is

Booster Pack The Dominance of God, set code OP-18, is the eighteenth main One Piece Card Game booster. Its Japanese title is 神の支配. Bandai's product pages say the set centres on Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God, with Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock making their card game debut.

Release date

Bandai's Japanese product page lists Saturday 21 November 2026. The English edition is listed for 20 November, which is why some Australian shops show the earlier date. Our pre-order is for the Japanese box.

What's in a box

  • 6 cards per pack (Bandai's Japanese product page).
  • 24 packs per box (the standard Japanese One Piece booster box; Bandai's page doesn't list a box count).
  • 127 card types plus 1, per Bandai. The rarity breakdown isn't published yet.

We'll leave pull rates alone until boxes are being opened. Judge the set on the printed list, not on hype.

Japanese vs English for this set

Japanese One Piece product ships first and uses Bandai's Japanese print run. The English edition is a separate product with a different pack size. If you want the broad comparison, our 2026 guide to Japanese Pokémon and One Piece booster boxes covers it. The spot-a-fake guide applies to Bandai boxes too: printed outer shrink with Bandai logos, one clean heat seal, matching set codes on every pack.
