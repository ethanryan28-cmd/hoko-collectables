What M7 is

Aura Seeker, set code M7, is the next Japanese expansion in the Mega Evolution series. Its Japanese name is Hadou Seeker (ハドウシーカー), and "Aura Seeker" is the English rendering most shops use. The headline card is reported to be a Mega Lucario ex, with Riolu and Shaymin ex also reported in the list. The full card list isn't out yet.

Release date

The Japanese release is reported as Friday 27 November 2026. Japanese card media expect The Pokémon Company to confirm the date around late October. Until then, treat 27 November as the working date, not a promise. We'll update this guide when the date is confirmed.
